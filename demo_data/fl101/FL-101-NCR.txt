1. WHAT HAPPENED
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

On the night shift of 16 January 2026, FL-101 raised underfill alarms
three times: at 21:40, 23:55 and 02:10.

After each alarm the operator increased the fill time to push more water
into the bottles: first from 3.2 to 3.6 seconds, then to 3.9 seconds.

The longer fill time made most valves overflow. Water spilled onto the
bottles and the conveyor, labels were damaged at the labeller, and the
line stopped twice to clean up spillage.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. TIMELINE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  21:40  First underfill alarm. Line stopped 15 minutes. Operator raised
         fill time 3.2 to 3.6 s and restarted.
  21:55  Underfill stopped. Overflow started on most valves.
  23:10  Line stopped 25 minutes to clean spillage from the conveyor.
  23:55  Second underfill alarm. Line stopped 15 minutes. Operator raised
         fill time to 3.9 s.
  01:20  Labeller reported wet, peeling labels. Line stopped 40 minutes.
  02:10  Third underfill alarm. Line stopped. Shift supervisor called.
  02:25  Supervisor found valves 7, 12, 13 and 19 dripping.
  02:45  No spare seal kits in the cabinet. Line stood down until morning.
         (Stops before this point: 15 + 25 + 15 + 40 + 35 minutes = 2 hours
         10 minutes.)
  06:30  Maintenance fitted seal kits from the main store. Fill time
         restored to 3.2 s.
  07:35  Rinse and first-bottle check passed. Line restarted.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
3. IMMEDIATE CONTAINMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  - All bottles filled between 21:40 and 02:10 were put on QA HOLD.
  - The quality lead checked samples from every pallet.
  - 1,200 bottles were rejected: overfilled, underfilled or with damaged
    labels.
  - Bottles within 495 to 505 ml with good labels were released.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
4. IMPACT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  Bottles rejected:    1,200
  Downtime:            2 hours 10 minutes during the shift
                       (plus the stop until morning waiting for parts)
  Labels wasted:       about 1,400
  Clean-up:            two conveyor clean-downs

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
5. ROOT CAUSE — FIVE WHYS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  Why were bottles rejected?
    Because most valves overflowed and four valves underfilled.
  Why did most valves overflow?
    Because the fill time was raised from 3.2 to 3.9 seconds.
  Why was the fill time raised?
    Because the operator tried to fix underfill without finding its cause.
  Why were four valves underfilling?
    Because their seals were worn out and dripping between fills.
  Why were the seals worn out?
    Because they were 900 running hours past the 1,500-hour replacement
    limit. The seal change had not been scheduled.

Root cause: the seals on four fill valves (valves 7, 12, 13 and 19) were
worn out. Raising the fill time did not fix the worn seals. It hid them for
a while and made the other 20 valves overfill.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
6. CONTRIBUTING CAUSES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  - The SOP did not say that operators must not change the fill time.
  - No spare seal kits were in stock on the line that night.
  - The seal replacement interval was not in the maintenance schedule.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
7. CORRECTIVE ACTIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  4    Set minimum stock of 10 SEAL-FV-24 kits in    Maintenance Sup.   25 Jan

8. LESSONS LEARNED
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  - Underfill is a seal problem until proven otherwise. Check for dripping
    valves first.
  - Never increase the fill time to fix underfill. It hides worn seals and
    causes overflow.
  - Three or more underfill alarms in one shift means a systemic fault.
    Stop and call maintenance.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
9. CHECK THAT THE FIX WORKED
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  - Three weeks after the actions, the checkweigher reject rate was below
    0.5 in 100 bottles on every shift.
  - No underfill alarm was caused by a seal past its replacement limit.
  - The fill time was checked on 10 random shifts and was 3.2 seconds
    every time.
