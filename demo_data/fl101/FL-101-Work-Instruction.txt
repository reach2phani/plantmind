FILL VALVE SEAL REPLACEMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. PURPOSE

This work instruction explains how to replace the seal kit in an FL-101
fill valve. A worn seal is the most common cause of underfill alarms.

2. SCOPE

Applies to all 24 fill valves on the FL-101 Bottle Filler. Only trained
maintenance technicians may carry out this work.

3. WHEN TO REPLACE THE SEALS

Replace the seal kit when:
  - A valve drips between fills
  - The checkweigher shows the same valve underfilling again and again
  - Three or more underfill alarms happen in one shift
  - The seal has reached its limit: 3 months or 1,500 running hours,
    whichever comes first

4. TIME NEEDED

  One valve:                     about 15 minutes
  A typical job (1 to 4 valves): 30 to 45 minutes
  Including rinse and check:     about 1 hour

5. PARTS AND TOOLS

  Seal kit, one per valve:  SEAL-FV-24 (main seal and O-ring)
  Food-grade lubricant:     FGL-1
  Location:                 Filling Line 1 spares cabinet, shelf 2
  Tools:                    valve key, seal pick, clean lint-free cloths,
                            20 empty 500 ml bottles for the check

6. SAFETY

  - Lock out the machine before starting (LOTO, SOP Section 6.2).
  - Wear safety glasses and gloves.
  - Only use food-grade lubricant. Any other lubricant contaminates the
    product.
  - Keep removed parts and tools on the tray. Nothing may be left inside
    the machine.

7. BEFORE YOU START

  - Check the checkweigher report to see which valves are underfilling.
  - Mark those valves with a green tag so the right valves are changed.
  - Tell the shift lead how long the line will be stopped.

8. PROCEDURE

  Step 1 — Press STOP and lock out the machine (LOTO).
  Step 2 — Drain the bowl until the product is below the valve level.
  Step 3 — Remove the valve with the valve key (quarter turn to the left).
  Step 4 — Remove the old seal and O-ring with the seal pick.
  Step 5 — Wipe the valve seat clean with a lint-free cloth. Check it for
           scratches. A scratched seat must be reported to the maintenance
           supervisor: a new seal will not seal on a scratched seat.
  Step 6 — Fit the new seal and O-ring from the SEAL-FV-24 kit, with a thin
           layer of FGL-1 lubricant.
  Step 7 — Refit the valve (quarter turn to the right until it clicks).
  Step 8 — Count the tools and parts on the tray. Remove the lock and refill
           the bowl.

  Step 9 — SANITATION RINSE — MANDATORY
           Run the sanitation rinse for 10 minutes. This removes lubricant
           and any particles from the new seals. Never skip this step: the
           first bottles would carry lubricant into the product.

  Step 10 — FIRST-BOTTLE CHECK — MANDATORY
           Run at normal speed and weigh the first 20 bottles.
           All 20 must be between 495 and 505 ml.
           If any bottle is outside this range, stop and check the valve again.

IMPORTANT: For the first 2 to 3 minutes after a seal change, bottles may be
slightly overfilled (up to 510 ml) while the new seals settle. This is
EXPECTED BEHAVIOUR — not a fault. Do not change any settings. The
checkweigher rejects these bottles automatically.

9. INSPECTING THE OLD SEALS

Look at every seal you remove. Signs of wear:
  - Flattened or squashed on one side
  - Small cracks, especially at the edge
  - Hard instead of soft and springy
  - A groove cut into the sealing face
  - Discoloured or swollen

Record what you found. If more than 4 seals are badly worn at the same
time, tell the maintenance supervisor: the replacement interval may be too
long.

10. TROUBLESHOOTING AFTER THE JOB

  What you see                        Check                     Action
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Valve still drips                   Seal seated correctly?    Refit the seal
  Valve still underfills, no drip     Nozzle blocked?           Clean the nozzle
  All valves underfill                Supply pressure?          SOP Section 12.4
  Overfill for 2 to 3 minutes only    Normal settling           No action
  Overfill after 5 minutes            Valve fully clicked in?   Refit the valve
  Leak at the valve body              O-ring missing?           Fit a new O-ring

11. SIGN-OFF

  - The shift supervisor must sign off the first-bottle check before normal
    production restarts.
  - Record in the maintenance log: date, valve numbers changed, seal kits
    used, condition of the old seals, first-bottle check results, and
    your name.
